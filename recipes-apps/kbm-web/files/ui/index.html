Mouse → right-stick tuning

Recoil & sensitivity

While the recoil action is held the daemon biases the right stick by (recoil X, recoil Y) to counter games' recoil. Sensitivity scales drop the right stick output when the ADS or fire actions are held, so the camera is less twitchy during precision aim or spray control.

Burst-on-hold

Oscillate a button or trigger while its source input is held. Useful for recoil-control: bind fire (r2) to a burst so click-and-hold pulses the trigger instead of holding it. hz=0 disables; duty is the fraction of each cycle the action is reported pressed (0..1, default 0.5).

The three realism knobs spread the cadence to look less machine-like. jitter (0..1) is the lognormal sigma on the interval — 0.25 gives a long-tailed distribution around the nominal rate. duty_jitter (0..1) does the same for the press duration. skip_prob (0..1) randomly drops a cycle's click entirely — ~0.05 models a human "I meant to click but didn't" miss every ~20 clicks. Reasonable Warzone starting point: jitter 0.25, duty_jitter 0.30, skip_prob 0.05.

Key / mouse mapping

Actions are named in DualSense terms (cross / circle / l2 / r2 / …). Because the Windows bridge translates this gadget to a virtual Xbox 360 controller, in-game prompts (Warzone etc.) show the Xbox equivalents — those are listed in parentheses next to each action name (e.g. cross (A), r2 (RT)).
